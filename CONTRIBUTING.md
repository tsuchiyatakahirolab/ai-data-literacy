# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they improve clarity, accessibility, reproducibility, or classroom usability.

Please keep the following principles.

1. Do not add real student data.
2. Keep core exercises tool-neutral whenever possible.
3. Place tool-specific instructions in `tool-guides/` or `current/`.
4. Avoid assignments that require paid accounts unless they are clearly marked as optional.
5. Keep beginner-level instructions explicit and short.

For substantial changes, open an issue first and explain the teaching problem the change addresses.
