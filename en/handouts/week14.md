| --- | --- | --- |

### 6. Ask AI to check the scope of your question

Copy this prompt, then add the specified source material:

```text
Can the sources I have answer this question?
Separate what these sources establish
from what needs additional sources.
If the question is too broad, narrow it to one question.
```

Give AI the question and sources. A suggestion alone does not add evidence.

### 7. Start your evidence log

Record how the claim and evidence connect. Long quotations are unnecessary.

### 12. Open the commit history

Open the **Code** tab in your repository. Above the file list, choose the clock icon and **Commits**. Look for a page headed **Commits**.

### 13. Choose the commit to submit

Find the change you saved and select its message. Check the date and the changes. The detail page shows **Commit** followed by a short identifier.

### 14. Check the files and copy the commit URL

1. Select **Browse files** and check the expected work under `week14/`.
2. Return to the commit detail page.
3. Select the full address with **Ctrl+L** on Windows or **Command+L** on Mac. Copy it with **Ctrl+C** or **Command+C**.
4. Check that the address includes `/commit/` and your own GitHub username.
5. In class, submit the full address through the course form under **Week 14** and check the confirmation page. For self-study, keep the address in your practice log.
