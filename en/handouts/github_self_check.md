# GitHub Hands-on Practice: Student Self-check

After the practice, check items 01–08 in order on your own screen. **Open the screen and mark only what you can actually confirm.** Reading or watching alone is not confirmation. Keep your existing repository and submissions.

[Practice guide](github_hands_on.md) / [PDF with cover](../slides/GitHub_Hands_On_EN.pdf) / [Videos](../../videos/github/README.md)

The PDF has 18 pages: one cover, 15 action steps, and two self-check pages. Action numbers 01–15 are not page numbers. Check your course page, instructor username, and practice week before starting.

For each item, choose either confirmed or not confirmed / unsure.

Mark a paper or local copy, or show the cue on your own screen to your instructor. You do not need to edit the public teaching material.

## 01  Sign-in and username

- **Open and do:** Open Profile and read the username in its URL.
- **Completion cue:** You can name your username.
- **Return to:** [Guide A](github_hands_on.md#a-reach-your-own-private-repository)
- [ ] Confirmed on my own screen.
- [ ] Not confirmed / unsure how to proceed: ask for help with this item number.

## 02  Your Private repository

- **Open and do:** Open Code in your own ai-learning repository.
- **Completion cue:** Own Owner, Private, folders present.
- **Return to:** [Guide A](github_hands_on.md#a-reach-your-own-private-repository)

If folders are missing, the Owner differs, or it is Public, preserve your work and ask your instructor. Do not delete and recreate it.

## 03  Setup image and short record

- **Open and do:** Open the setup image and setup/README.md separately.
- **Completion cue:** The image opens and your record is readable. Use the setup guide.
- **Return to:** [Initial setup](setup.md)
- [ ] Confirmed on my own screen.
- [ ] Not confirmed / unsure how to proceed: ask for help with this item number.

Open the actual image file and separately read the short use record in setup/README.md. Image quality and paid plans are not conditions.

## 04  Instructor invitation

- **Open and do:** Settings → Collaborators: check the specified instructor.
- **Completion cue:** Describe pending or accepted.
- **Return to:** [Guide C, item 1](github_hands_on.md#c-invite-the-instructor-and-submit-a-specific-version)
- [ ] Confirmed on my own screen.
- [ ] Not confirmed / unsure how to proceed: ask for help with this item number.

Pending means you sent the invitation. Instructor access is checked separately after acceptance. Do not invite again if already accepted.

## 05  Saved README

- **Open and do:** Reopen this week’s README.md and read your added text.
- **Completion cue:** Your text remains outside the editor.
- **Return to:** [Guide B](github_hands_on.md#b-edit-save-and-reopen-readme)
- [ ] Confirmed on my own screen.
- [ ] Not confirmed / unsure how to proceed: ask for help with this item number.

If you have not practiced saving, keep the existing headings and work, add one true checking record, save, and reopen the file.

## 06  Files in the submitted version

- **Open and do:** Latest commit → Browse files: open all required weekly files.
- **Completion cue:** All required files are present.
- **Return to:** [Guide C, items 3–5](github_hands_on.md#c-invite-the-instructor-and-submit-a-specific-version)
- [ ] Confirmed on my own screen.
- [ ] Not confirmed / unsure how to proceed: ask for help with this item number.

Save missing files and use the new final commit. After Browse files, return to the /commit/ page before copying its URL.

## If an item is incomplete

Say, for example, “Self-check 06: I cannot find Commits,” with the item number and visible message. If you cannot sign in, ask for help with item 01 first; do not mark later items as completed. Keep passwords and sign-in codes private. This sheet checks your operations, not grades or instructor check results. No public student upload or new verification form is required. Independent readers may mark course-specific items 03, 04, 07, and 08 as not applicable; class participants follow their instructor’s instructions.
