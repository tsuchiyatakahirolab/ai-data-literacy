### 4. 1. A short request

Copy this prompt, then add the specified source material:

```text
Write a notice for a university AI workshop.
```

Check whether AI invented the date, location or fee.

### 5. Details AI may not know

Missing from the request

Who should read it?  
When and where is it?  
What should they bring?

Material to add

The audience is new students.  
Use the supplied information.  
Aim for about 180 characters.

### 6. 2. A source and clear conditions

Copy this prompt, then add the specified source material:

```text
Use the supplied material to write a notice
for new students in about 180 characters.
Include the date, venue, items to bring and deadline.
Add no information absent from the source.
```

Paste the supplied source text after this request.

### 7. Conditions that matter for this task

Purpose

Who is the text for?  
What should it communicate?

Source

Which material should AI use?  
Which facts must stay fixed?

Output

Length, required details  
and completion criteria.

Choose the conditions your task needs. A long fixed prompt template is unnecessary.

### 8. Comparing the same items

Record what worked as well. There is no need to manufacture a bad result.

### 9. A focused correction

Copy this prompt, then add the specified source material:

```text
Keep the date and venue unchanged.
Add university account to the items to bring.
Keep all other information unchanged.
```

If the first result meets the conditions, additional attempts are unnecessary.

## Save and submit

Save the artifact and a short AI-use record in `week02/README.md`, then commit all required files. Submit the **final commit URL** through the course form. Initial registration uses the repository URL; weekly and final submissions use a commit URL.

Do not include student IDs, university emails, passwords, or confidential source material. Full AI chat histories are not required. Self-study readers can complete the exercise without university forms.

Character limits in prompts follow the source exercise. Follow any language-specific length specified by your instructor.
