# Final-project packet

Choose one approach.

## A. Document reading

Read one public or instructor-provided document. Use AI to summarize it, organize the issues, and check supporting evidence.

## B. Research

Use AI-assisted search to investigate one question and check at least two sources. Record where AI’s explanation agrees or disagrees with the originals.

## C. Data

Use a CSV or table. Ask AI for summaries and a chart, then distinguish what the data support from what they cannot establish.

## Common requirements

- Produce an 800–1,200-character artifact or an equivalent one-page document, following any language-specific length set by the instructor.
- Record what you delegated to AI, what you checked yourself, and unresolved issues.
- Submit the final commit URL through the Final Form.

The assessment weights are unchanged: artifact 50%, verification and revision 25%, AI-use record 15%, GitHub submission 10%.
