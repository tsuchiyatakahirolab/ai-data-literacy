# Week 13: Delegate a task with several steps

Five university students plan a half-day visit in Kyoto, from 13:00 to 17:30, starting and ending at Kyoto Station. Use walking and public transport, with a transport budget of JPY 1,500 per person. Include one quiet place and one place for a short break.

Delegate the organization of constraints, candidate places, route order, estimated duration, and a comparison table to AI. Check actual opening days and hours, fares, travel times, crowding, reservations, and any proposal that does not meet the constraints.

Use the [offline exercise data](offline_itinerary_data.md) when practicing without live research. Its facilities, travel times and fares are fictional and must not be used as travel advice.
