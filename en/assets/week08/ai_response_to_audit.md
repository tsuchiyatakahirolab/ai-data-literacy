# Week 08: Audit an AI response

The following fictional answer contains unsupported claims, overgeneralization, and excessive certainty. Identify the problems and rewrite it more carefully.

## Fictional AI answer

University students will always improve their grades if they use generative AI. This is because AI is more accurate than humans and can provide the best answer to any assignment. Universities should therefore require AI use in every assignment. Students who do not use AI are behind the times and can be assumed to have low data literacy.

## Questions to check

- Which parts can be established as facts?
- Which judgments lack supporting evidence?
- Which phrases stereotype the reader unnecessarily?
- Under what conditions might a narrower claim be valid?
