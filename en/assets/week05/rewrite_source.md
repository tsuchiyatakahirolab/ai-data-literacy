# Week 05: Sample text for rewriting

This is a fictional text for teaching.

Generative AI can help university students organize information, draft text, and understand documents. However, its output may contain errors or lack supporting evidence. When using AI in class, students need to record which AI they used and for what purpose, and check the final content themselves.

## Rewriting conditions

1. Explain this in plain English for first-year university students.
2. Explain it to parents or guardians as a course policy.
3. Translate it into another language that you can verify, preserving the meaning and conditions.

If you cannot verify another language, agree on an alternative audience-focused English rewrite with your instructor. Record that the translation was not verified; do not claim that a second AI’s agreement proves it correct.
