# Week 12: Use AI as a tutor

## Topic

The difference between correlation and causation.

## Example prompt

```text
Explain the difference between correlation and causation to a first-year university student. Then give me three questions to check my understanding. Wait for my answers, then explain what I understood and what needs more work.
```

Record what was clear, what you answered incorrectly, and how reading the feedback changed your understanding. Check the explanation against your course material or an appropriate original source.
