# Week 06: Artifact brief

## Situation

Explain precautions for using generative AI in class to first-year university students.

## Readers

Students who have tried generative AI but are unfamiliar with source checking and GitHub submission.

## Format

Choose a 300–500-character explanation, a one-page handout, or a short notice that can be read at the start of class. The character range mirrors the source task; follow any language-specific length set by your instructor.

## Required content

- AI use is allowed within the course policy.
- Students must take responsibility for the final work.
- Record the AI used and the checks made in GitHub.
