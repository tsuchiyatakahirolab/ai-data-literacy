# Week 01: Ten ways to try AI beyond chat

Try at least three in class.

1. Ask AI to explain an unfamiliar concept.
2. Ask it to explain the same concept to a high-school student.
3. Give it an image and ask it to describe the contents.
4. Give it a PDF or document and ask for three main points.
5. Ask it to research current information on the web.
6. Open and check the cited sources yourself.
7. Give it a table and ask for an average or percentage.
8. Ask it to create a chart.
9. Ask it for an idea for an image.
10. Ask it to plan a task with several steps.

Finish by distinguishing tasks you can delegate to AI from tasks you must check yourself.
