# AI and Data Literacy — English edition

Start with the [browser setup guide](handouts/setup.md), then [Week 01](handouts/week01.md). No programming or command-line Git is required. Independent readers can follow the [self-study guide](docs/self_study.md) without university forms.

- [16 editable PowerPoint decks and slide-only PDFs](slides/README.md): initial setup, 14 weeks, and the final project; 250 slides with English teaching notes.
- [All 250 slides in one PDF](slides/AI_Data_Literacy_All_Slides_EN.pdf), with no speaker notes.
- [Instructor runbook](docs/instructor_runbook.md), [assessment rubric](docs/assessment_rubric.md), [AI-use policy](docs/ai_use_policy.md), and [submission workflow](docs/submission_workflow.md).
- [English course overview](../README_EN.md), [final project](handouts/final_project.md), and [instructor adaptation guide](docs/instructor_adaptation.md).
- [English blank student template](https://github.com/tsuchiyatakahirolab/ai-data-literacy-student-template-en). Students create a Private copy with Use this template.
- [English teaching ZIP](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tsuchiyatakahirolab/ai-data-literacy/main/downloads/AI_Data_Literacy_Materials_EN.zip) and [complete bilingual ZIP](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tsuchiyatakahirolab/ai-data-literacy/main/downloads/AI_Data_Literacy_Materials.zip).

Each hands-on sheet follows the slides and includes copyable prompts. English practice materials are in `assets/`. The sample data and Kyoto itinerary are fictional teaching exercises. The original Japanese materials remain available in the [Japanese edition](../README.md).

The English Week 05 exercise translates an English source into another language the learner can verify. Follow your instructor’s policy for language-specific length limits. Assessment weights and numerical examples are shared with the Japanese edition.

Your instructor supplies the course account, instructor username, forms, deadlines, and feedback method. Screenshots and fictional usernames are examples. Some setup screenshots show the Japanese source template; the English template uses English input fields.
