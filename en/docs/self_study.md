# Using this material independently

You do not need an account at any particular university, access to a course submission form, or a paid AI subscription. Start with the English course notes and use an AI service available to you. [English PowerPoint decks, slide-only PDFs](../slides/README.md), [hands-on sheets](../handouts/week01.md), and English practice materials are available. Start with the [setup guide](../handouts/setup.md), then Week 01.

Save your output and a short record of what you checked. Use a private GitHub repository to practice version control, or a local folder when trying only the AI activities. Independent readers do not need to invite an instructor or submit a Google Form. Students enrolled in a taught course must follow their instructor's submission requirements instead.

Materials labeled as synthetic or fictional are teaching fixtures, not evidence about real students or actual travel services. Preserve these labels when reusing the material.

Instructors can use the [adaptation guide](instructor_adaptation.md) to prepare their own course information.
