# Fictional itinerary fixture for Week 13

All places, opening hours, fares and travel times below are fictional teaching assumptions. Do not use them for a real trip.

Five students leave Kyoto Station at 13:00 and must return by 17:30. The transport budget is JPY 1,500 per person. Include one quiet venue and a break of at least 30 minutes. Admissions are free in this exercise, food purchases are unnecessary, and no booking or payment is allowed. Waiting time is included in each journey time.

| Place | Available time | Minimum stay |
|---|---|---|
| Museum A | 13:00–17:00 | 45 minutes; counts as the quiet venue |
| Park B | 13:00–18:00 | 30 minutes |
| Rest area C | 14:00–17:00 | 30 minutes; counts as the break |

| Route, either direction | Time | Fare per person |
|---|---:|---:|
| Station–Museum A | 30 min | JPY 230 |
| Museum A–Rest area C | 20 min | JPY 230 |
| Rest area C–Park B | 15 min | JPY 230 |
| Park B–Station | 30 min | JPY 230 |

Use only these routes, combining them if necessary. A valid example is departure at 13:00, Museum A at 13:30–14:15, Rest area C at 14:35–15:05, Park B at 15:20–15:50, and return at 16:20. Transport costs JPY 920 per person.

Next, extend the museum stay to 60 minutes and recheck every constraint. The same route returns at 16:35 at the same fare. Other plans are acceptable if all assumptions and constraints are met.
