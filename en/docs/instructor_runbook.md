# Instructor runbook

Start with the [adaptation guide](instructor_adaptation.md) and place the required links on your course page.

## Before teaching

1. Choose the lessons and hands-on sheets.
2. Prepare an empty student template, your instructor GitHub username, and submission destinations.
3. Link the setup guide, weekly materials, initial registration, weekly forms, and final-project submission on the course page.
4. Specify the sign-in accounts and student-number format.
5. Check access, form entry, and feedback under the conditions students will use.

Check existing submission destinations before using them. Keep personal information and course-only links out of the public material.

## Initial setup

Use the [setup guide](../handouts/setup.md). Check AI access, the GitHub username, each student's own Private repository, the image and short record, the instructor invitation, and initial registration. Students who already created a repository keep it.

## Weekly work and support

Students save their artifact and short AI-use record, then submit the final commit URL. Collect the course information you need, such as student email, week, commit URL, AI used, and checking or revision. Full AI conversations are not mandatory.

For access problems, check the required sign-in account. For student-number problems, follow the university format and form instructions. Find commit URLs in repository history. Correct files in the same repository; correct registration through the course's stated method. Never ask students to reveal passwords or sign-in codes.

## Checking and assessment

Check registration details, repository access, the image, and the short record. Distinguish waiting for a check from an incomplete setup, and state how results are delivered. Late joiners use the same setup guide; communicate earlier deadlines under your course policy.

Assess artifacts, evidence checking and revision, AI-use records, and submission separately using the [rubric](assessment_rubric.md) and the weights published for your course.
