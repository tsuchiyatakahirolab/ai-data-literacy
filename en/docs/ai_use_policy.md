# AI-use policy

Students may use an AI service available to them, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Microsoft Copilot, NotebookLM, Perplexity, or GitHub Copilot. An institution-provided service may be recommended. Design exercises so that they do not depend on one particular service.

Each time, record the AI or capability used, the work delegated to AI, and what you checked or revised yourself. Full conversations are normally not required. Conversation-sharing links are not mandatory because sharing depends on subscriptions, institutional settings, and attachments.

Do not send AI other people's personal information, such as names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, and student numbers; sensitive information such as grades, health, or family circumstances that is unnecessary for the exercise; confidential university or workplace materials; or information whose terms prohibit sending it to external AI services.

Check sources, numbers, dates, names, chart axes, units, and the scope of the evidence. When you cannot verify something, record that limitation.
