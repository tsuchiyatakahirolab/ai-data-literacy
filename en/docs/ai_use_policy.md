# AI Use Policy

Students may use AI in this course. The course is designed for active AI use. However, AI use must be recorded, checked, and revised by the student.

Students should record which AI service or function they used, what they delegated to AI, what they checked themselves, and what they changed before submission. Full chat histories are not normally required.

Students must not input personal information about themselves or others, non-public course materials, confidential documents, passwords, API keys, or data they do not have permission to share.

The final artifact is the student's responsibility. A claim is not supported merely because AI produced it. For source-based assignments, students must open and check the relevant source, date, number, and wording.
