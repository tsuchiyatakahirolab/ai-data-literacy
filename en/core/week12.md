# Week 12: Using AI as a study partner

[Japanese edition](../../core/week12.md)

## Learning objective

Use AI as a study partner for explanation, quizzes, and error analysis while checking explanations against course material.

## Guiding question

AI can be more useful as a study partner than as an answer machine. Use it to reveal gaps in your own understanding.

## Materials

- [`assets/week12/ai_tutor_case.md`](../assets/week12/ai_tutor_case.md)

## In-class activity

1. Choose one concept you find difficult and ask AI to explain it.
2. Ask AI to create a short quiz and answer without consulting the source.
3. Review mistakes with AI and verify the explanation against course material or another reliable source.
