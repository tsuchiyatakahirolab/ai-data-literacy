# Week 07: Researching with AI and checking sources

[日本語](../../core/week07.md)

## Learning objective

Use AI-assisted search as a starting point, then open sources and verify publisher, date, numbers, names, and meaning.

## Guiding question

A citation does not guarantee that a source supports the claim. Open the source and compare the original text with the AI answer.

## Materials

- [`assets/week07/source_check_template.md`](../../assets/week07/source_check_template.md)

## In-class activity

1. Ask AI to research a current public-information question.
2. Select two claims and open the cited sources.
3. Record whether the publisher, date, numbers, names, and meaning of the source support the AI answer.
