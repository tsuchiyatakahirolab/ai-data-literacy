# Final Project: Produce an evidence-based artifact with AI

[日本語](../../core/final_project.md)

## Objective

Combine the practices used throughout the course: AI-assisted work, document reading, source checking, basic numerical or chart verification when relevant, and GitHub-based work records. You are assessed on whether you can produce an evidence-based artifact and explain what you delegated to AI and what you verified or revised yourself.

## Task

Choose one question and use AI to support research, document reading, data checking when relevant, and production of a final artifact. Topics may come from the course, university life, international affairs, economics, society, or study methods. Do not use personal information, confidential material, or real student data.

## Deliverables

1. In `final/README.md`, state your question, AI service(s), what you delegated to AI, what you checked or revised yourself, and any unresolved issues.
2. Place the final artifact in `final/`. It may be a short report, one-page handout, table, chart, or equivalent artifact specified by your instructor.
3. Keep an evidence log in `final/evidence_log.md` or as a table in the README.
4. Submit the commit URL that contains the final project through the Final Form.

## Assessment

- Artifact: 50% — answers the question and is understandable to its intended reader.
- Verification and revision: 25% — checks sources, numbers, dates, names, and changes in meaning when relevant.
- AI-use record: 15% — explains what AI did and what the student judged or revised.
- GitHub submission: 10% — stores the work in the required location and submits a commit URL.

## Final checklist

- [ ] The question is written in one sentence.
- [ ] The README makes clear what AI did and what I checked myself.
- [ ] Relevant claims, numbers, dates, and names have been checked.
- [ ] No personal or confidential information is included.
- [ ] `final/README.md`, the evidence log, and the artifact are committed.
- [ ] The final commit URL has been submitted.
