# Week 04: Reading images, PDFs, and tables with AI

[日本語](../../core/week04.md)

## Learning objective

Give AI a source document and distinguish claims supported by that source from claims that require other evidence.

## Guiding question

AI can read documents, but it may add details that are not present. Compare its explanation with the source and identify the boundary of the evidence.

## Materials

- [`assets/week04/sample_event_notice.pdf`](../../assets/week04/sample_event_notice.pdf)

## In-class activity

1. Give AI the assigned image or PDF and ask for a short explanation.
2. Ask it to answer only from the supplied material and compare the result.
3. Check each important statement against the original material and separate supported from unsupported claims.
