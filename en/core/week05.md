# Week 05: Summarizing, translating, and rewriting

[日本語](../../core/week05.md)

## Learning objective

Transform text for different readers and purposes while checking that meaning, conditions, numbers, and names remain accurate.

## Guiding question

Summaries and translations can become easier to read while quietly changing conditions or meaning. Practice transforming text without losing what matters.

## Materials

- [`assets/week05/rewrite_source.md`](../../assets/week05/rewrite_source.md)

## In-class activity

1. Summarize or translate the assigned source text.
2. Rewrite the same content for a different audience.
3. Compare the versions and verify that meaning, numbers, conditions, and proper nouns have not changed.
