# Week 14: Preparing the final project

[日本語](../../core/week14.md)

## Learning objective

Prepare the final project by defining the question, sources, verification plan, artifact format, and GitHub submission process.

## Guiding question

The final project rewards an evidence-based artifact, not the amount of AI used. Plan verification before you begin.

## Materials

- [`assets/week14/final_planning_template.md`](../../assets/week14/final_planning_template.md)

## In-class activity

1. Write the final-project question in one sentence and narrow its scope.
2. Identify the sources or data you will use and separate AI tasks from human verification tasks.
3. Plan the final artifact, evidence log, and GitHub submission.

## Assignment

Complete the activity above and save the required artifact together with a short AI-use record in your course repository.

## What to record in your README

```markdown
# Week 14: Preparing the final project

## Artifact
Link to or place the artifact produced for this week.

## AI service(s) used
List the service(s). If you do not know the model name, write “unknown.”

## AI capability used
For example: explanation, web research, document reading, image understanding, data analysis, image generation/editing, voice, or multi-step task execution.

## What I delegated to AI
Describe the task given to AI in 2–4 sentences.

## What I checked or revised myself
Describe what you verified and what you changed in 3–5 sentences.

## Remaining issues
Note anything you could not verify or complete.
```

## Submission

Update `week14/README.md`, add any required files to the same folder, commit the changes, and submit the **commit URL** through the course form.
