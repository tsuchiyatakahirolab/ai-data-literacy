# Week 09: Working with numbers

[Japanese edition](../../core/week09.md)

## Learning objective

Check percentages, averages, differences, denominators, and units and explain what the numbers do and do not support.

## Guiding question

Numbers look authoritative, but a wrong denominator or unit changes the conclusion. Verify the calculation and its meaning.

## Materials

- [`assets/week09/numbers_exercise.csv`](../assets/week09/numbers_exercise.csv)

## In-class activity

1. Give AI the provided table or CSV and ask for the required calculation.
2. Check the formula, denominator, units, and source values yourself.
3. State what the result supports and what the data cannot establish.
