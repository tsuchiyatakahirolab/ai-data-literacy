# Week 13: Delegating a multi-step task to AI

[日本語](../../core/week13.md)

## Learning objective

Break a multi-step task into stages and manage which parts AI performs and which parts require human verification.

## Guiding question

As AI handles longer workflows, you need to decide where human checks belong. Manage the process, not just the final answer.

## Materials

- [`assets/week13/multi_step_planning_case.md`](../../assets/week13/multi_step_planning_case.md)

## In-class activity

1. Break the case into smaller steps and ask AI to propose an execution order.
2. Use AI to research, compare, and draft a plan.
3. Check constraints, dates, evidence, and feasibility, then revise the plan.
