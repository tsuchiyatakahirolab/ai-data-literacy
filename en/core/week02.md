# Week 02: Giving AI a clear task

[日本語](../../core/week02.md)

## Learning objective

Improve an ambiguous request by adding the purpose, relevant input, desired output, and verification criteria when they are useful.

## Guiding question

You do not need a memorized “magic prompt.” Compare what happens when you give AI the information it actually needs to complete a task.

## Materials

- [`assets/week02/bad_and_better_requests.md`](../../assets/week02/bad_and_better_requests.md)

## In-class activity

1. Give AI a short, ambiguous request and save the result.
2. Add the information it actually needs: purpose, relevant material, desired format, and/or verification criteria.
3. Compare the two outputs and explain what improved.
