# Week 01: Mapping the AI landscape

[日本語](../../core/week01.md)

## Learning objective

Experience AI as more than a chatbot by trying capabilities for reading, seeing, researching, analyzing, creating, and completing tasks.

## Guiding question

What can AI do besides answer questions? Try several capabilities and distinguish convenience from tasks that still require human checking.

## Materials

- [`assets/week01/ai_try_10.md`](../../assets/week01/ai_try_10.md)

## In-class activity

1. Try at least three AI capabilities, such as explanation, web research, image understanding, document reading, data analysis, or image generation.
2. For each capability, note what AI can reasonably handle and what you should verify yourself.
3. Choose one capability that seems useful for your studies and explain why.

## Assignment

Complete the activity above and save the required artifact together with a short AI-use record in your course repository.

## What to record in your README

```markdown
# Week 01: Mapping the AI landscape

## Artifact

## AI capability used
For example: explanation, web research, document reading, image understanding, data analysis, image generation/editing, voice, or multi-step task execution.

## What I delegated to AI
Describe the task given to AI in 2–4 sentences.

## What I checked or revised myself
Describe what you verified and what you changed in 3–5 sentences.

## Remaining issues
Note anything you could not verify or complete.
```
