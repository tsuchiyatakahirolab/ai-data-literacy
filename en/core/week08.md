# Week 08: Finding errors and bias in AI output

[Japanese edition](../../core/week08.md)

## Learning objective

Identify factual errors, weak evidence, overgeneralization, excessive certainty, and biased wording, then revise the output.

## Guiding question

Fluent writing can still contain unsupported claims. Evaluate the relationship between claims, evidence, and certainty rather than style alone.

## Materials

- [`assets/week08/ai_response_to_audit.md`](../assets/week08/ai_response_to_audit.md)

## In-class activity

1. Read the provided AI response and mark statements that need verification.
2. Classify problems such as factual error, weak evidence, overgeneralization, or biased wording.
3. Check the necessary evidence and produce a corrected version with appropriate certainty.
